# WarrenWise Youth Animal Training Academy - Accuracy & Legal Policy

## 1. Core Educational Position
WarrenWise Youth Animal Training Academy is an independent educational training platform.
- **Position**: Educational training app first.
- **Not a pedigree registry**: Breeding, sales contracts, or herd registration transactions belong in herd management tools (such as RabbitryPedigree Pro).
- **Not an official 4-H certification replacement**: The Academy does not issue official USDA, National 4-H Council, ARBA, ACBA, or state cooperative extension certificates.

## 2. Non-Endorsement & Legal Disclaimers
1. **No False Claims**: The application, its branding, documentation, and promotional materials strictly avoid claiming official endorsement, sponsorship, or partnership with:
   - National 4-H Council / USDA National Institute of Food and Agriculture (NIFA)
   - American Rabbit Breeders Association (ARBA)
   - American Cavy Breeders Association (ACBA)
   - Youth for the Quality Care of Animals (YQCA)
   - Any state or county Cooperative Extension Service
2. **Distinct Recognition**:
   - **App Mastery Badges & Certificates**: Recognize academic achievement within this platform only.
   - **Official Credentials**: Youth exhibitors are directed to their local county Extension office or fair board for official exhibition credentials and weigh-in certifications.
3. **Local Rules Notice**: Fairs, show regulations, age cutoffs, and weigh-in rules vary significantly across states and counties. Learners are consistently instructed to verify local fair books and club requirements.

## 3. Strict Veterinary & Animal Health Boundaries
1. **Observation & Prevention Only**:
   - Content instructs youth on daily health checks (eyes, ears, teeth, hocks, vent, weight).
   - Content emphasizes biosecurity principles (30-day quarantine, sanitizing, dedicated boots).
2. **Absolute Veterinary Refusal Boundary**:
   - Neither the curriculum, the quiz banks, nor the WarrenWise Trainer AI may prescribe medications, antibiotics, antiparasitics, or calculate drug dosages.
   - When disease symptoms or emergencies are detected (e.g., severe lethargy, white nasal discharge, bloat, heat prostration), youth must immediately:
     1. Isolate the animal in a safe, quiet quarantine pen.
     2. Report to an adult leader or parent.
     3. Consult a licensed veterinarian or local Extension specialist.

## 4. Content Originality & Copyright Protection
- Curriculum modules and quiz questions are 100% original works based on public biological facts, accepted husbandry science, and standard showmanship procedures.
- No copyrighted manuals, proprietary quiz bowls, or private club exams are copied or reproduced.
